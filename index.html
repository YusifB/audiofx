Privacy Policy for Audio Media Player EQ

Developer: Nexus Interactive JD

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Audio Media Player EQ application (referred to as "the App"), developed and operated by Nexus Interactive JD. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App. The App is designed to provide audio tuning (equalizer), media playback, and audio recording features. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. While we do not directly collect personally identifiable information, we utilize third-party analytics tools to gather non-identifying usage and regional data to improve our services.

2. Device Permissions and Data Handling

To provide core features, Audio Media Player EQ requires specific device permissions:

  • Microphone (Record Audio): Required exclusively for the audio recorder feature. The audio you record is processed and saved locally on your device. We do not upload, listen to, or store your voice recordings on any remote servers.
  • Storage (Media Access): Required to access your device's audio files for the media player and equalizer features, and to save your recordings. We do not access photos, videos, or personal files unrelated to the app's core audio functionality.

3. Data Collection and Analytics

All core audio processing in Audio Media Player EQ happens locally on your device, and we do not require you to create an account. However, to understand our audience and enhance the App, we collect anonymous analytics data. This includes general location information (such as the country you are in) and app usage patterns. This data is used solely for analysis, allowing us to see performance metrics and improve user experience.

4. Third-Party Services

Audio Media Player EQ uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include analytics tools like Google Analytics for Firebase and advertising networks to support app development, monitor crashes, and analyze user engagement. These services automatically collect data such as device identifiers, operating system details, and geographic location (at a country or region level). Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more details.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. Nexus Interactive JD does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
