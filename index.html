Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data. Our app is designed to provide audio tuning (equalizer), media playback, and audio recording features. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. While we do not directly collect personally identifiable information, we utilize analytics tools to gather non-identifying usage and regional data to improve our services.

2. Device Permissions and Data Handling

To provide our core features, the app requires specific device permissions. Here is how we use them:

  • Microphone (Record Audio): Required exclusively for the audio recorder feature. The audio you record is processed and saved locally on your device. We do not upload, listen to, or store your voice recordings on any remote servers.
  • Storage (Media Access): Required to access your device's audio files for the media player and equalizer features, and to save your recordings. We do not access photos, videos, or personal files unrelated to the app's core audio functionality.

3. Data Collection and Analytics

All core audio processing happens locally on your device, and we do not require you to create an account. However, to understand our audience and enhance the app, we collect anonymous analytics data. This includes general location information (such as the country you are in) and app usage patterns. This data is used solely for market analysis, allowing us to see where our traffic comes from and how we can better serve our primary markets.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services, including analytics tools like Google Analytics for Firebase and advertising networks, to support app development, monitor crashes, and analyze user engagement. These services automatically collect data such as device identifiers, operating system details, and geographic location (at a country or region level). Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information on how they handle this data.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
