Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data. Our app is designed to provide audio tuning (equalizer), media playback, and audio recording features. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. We do not collect or share your personal data.

2. Device Permissions and Data Handling

To provide our core features, the app requires specific device permissions. Here is how we use them:

  • Microphone (Record Audio): Required exclusively for the audio recorder feature. The audio you record is processed and saved locally on your device. We do not upload, listen to, or store your voice recordings on any remote servers.
  • Storage (Media Access): Required to access your device's audio files for the media player and equalizer features, and to save your recordings. We do not access photos, videos, or personal files unrelated to the app's core audio functionality.

3. Data Collection and Usage

All audio processing happens locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or location).

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services (such as advertising networks or analytics tools like Google AdMob / Firebase) to support the app's development and monitor crashes. These services may collect anonymous usage data and device identifiers. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
